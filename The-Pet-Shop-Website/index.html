Choose a Pet!

Finding the right pet is an important and exciting decision.

Visit the ASPCA pet care guide to learn more about responsible pet care.

Cats & Kittens

Cats are playful companions that enjoy comfortable spaces and daily attention.

Dogs & Puppies

Dogs are loyal friends that need exercise, training, and plenty of care.

Rabbits

Rabbits are gentle pets that need a safe home, fresh hay, and room to move.

Guinea Pigs

Guinea pigs are social animals that enjoy company and a healthy daily diet.

Fish

Fish need a clean aquarium with suitable water, food, and space for their species.

Reptiles

Reptiles need a carefully prepared habitat with the correct heat and lighting.
